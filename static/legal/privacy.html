Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

This explains what [Company name] ("GridSim") collects, why, and who else handles it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile Google shares. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are not readable by us.
  • Leagues you connect: the platform, league ID, league name, which team is yours, and for private ESPN leagues the espn_s2 and SWID cookies you paste in (stored encrypted).
  • Billing: whether you have a plan and when it ends. Card details go to Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Technical: standard server logs (IP address, time, pages requested) used for security and fixing problems.

How we use it

To sign you in, read your leagues, show your projections, take payment, keep the service secure, and answer support questions. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase: sign-in and our database.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • [Hosting provider, for example Fly.io]: runs the servers.
  • ESPN and Sleeper: when you open a league, our server asks them for that league's data on your behalf.

Keeping and deleting data

Remove a league in Settings and its saved details, including any stored cookies, are deleted. To delete your account and everything we hold, email [support email]. Billing records may be kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Email [support email] and we will respond within [30] days.
